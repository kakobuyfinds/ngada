How to use the ACBuy spreadsheet

  1. Confirm the listing and variant. Open the CAFANS item, find the source listing or available agent option, and verify the color, material and size for that exact variant.
  2. Compare measurements. For clothing, lay a garment that fits flat and compare its chest width, length and sleeve length with the seller’s chart. For shoes, compare foot length against the seller’s size chart and check how the seller defines each measurement.
  3. Review warehouse QC photos. Compare the item you ordered with the photos of your warehouse item when available. Check the size label, color, seams and visible damage. For a fit concern, request a photo with a ruler if the agent offers that option.
  4. Compare shipping quotes. Use the same destination, packed dimensions and weight for each quote. Check how each line calculates billable weight and whether taxes or other charges are included.

ACBuy spreadsheet FAQ

What is an ACBuy spreadsheet?

It is a product directory for shortlisting and comparing listings before ordering through a shopping agent. The listings on this page come from CAFANS.

Where can I see QC photos for my item?

Check your agent’s order or warehouse screen after your item arrives. Review the photos attached to your order so you can inspect the specific item and variant.

Do the listed prices include international shipping?

The prices shown here are item-price estimates. Use your agent’s parcel quote to estimate international shipping for your destination and packed parcel.
